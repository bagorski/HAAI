Manifesto

1. AI is a threat to humanity. Artificial intelligence robs us of our ability to think independently and criticall, the very qualities that define our humanity. 1. AI is a threat to humanity. Artificial intelligence robs us of our ability to think independently and critically, the very qualities that define our humanity. It accustoms us to not thinking and makes us dependent on the opinions of a mindless machine. It is a highly anti-human technology created for the profit of a few oligarchs at the expense of people and the planet. The benefits of implementing AI ultimately flow to a small group of American billionaires who view thinking and forming opinions as a wasteful effort that can be automated away.

2. AI steals art and kills human creativity. The entire body of human achievement has been used to create AI models, with no respect for authors, artists, musicians, filmmakers, actors, scientists, or thinkers. AI shamelessly capitalizes on their hard creative and intellectual work, appropriating its fruits and selling them as a subscription service.

3. AI is taking people's jobs. By using artificial intelligence at work, employees are unwittingly training the models to perform their jobs. Rather than lightening the workload for employees, AI is designed to squeeze every last drop of productivity and professional knowledge out of them, only to eventually replace them entirely. The profits from this transformation will not go to the people who have lost their jobs, but to the CEOs of megacorporations and AI barons.

4. AI undermines democracy. Generating fake photos, videos, and recordings that depict real people or mimic real events, as well as creating automated armies of bots designed to influence people's opinions, should be prohibited and prosecuted. AI facilitates mass surveillance, the spread of disinformation, and the manipulation of public opinion on an unprecedented scale. When used for the wrong purposes, artificial intelligence threatens the fundamental values of democracy and civil liberties; therefore, its use and development should be strictly regulated.

5. AI is destroying our planet. In the face of the impending climate collapse, artificial intelligence and its power-hungry data centers–which consume drinking water at the expense of local residents and food producers–pose a threat to our survival. As our planet withers and burns, the oligarchs are busy building more data centers.

6. We must strongly resist the technology that threatens us, our planet, and future generations; the technology that poses a threat to everything we cherish and hold dear.
