Our Objectives and Statute

Objectives of the Foundation

In the faces of advances in artificial intelligence technology the Foundation aims to:

  1. Work to protect:
    1. human rights, human dignity and personal image,
    2. culture, the art, and artists,
    3. workers' rights and jobs,
    4. the right to access reliable information and education,
    5. consumer rights,
    6. the natural environment.
  2. Raise public awareness of the risks posed by the development of artificial intelligence technology, and in particular its use for the purpose of:
    1. mass surveillance, social manipulation, and information warfare,
    2. identity theft,
    3. deceptive advertising,
    4. financial fraud,
    5. criminal activity.
  3. Educate the public about the negative impact of artificial intelligence technology on human cognitive abilities and the natural environment.
  4. Support the development of ethical technologies that serve the common good.
